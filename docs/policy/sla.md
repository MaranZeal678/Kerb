# Service Level Agreements

Claims acknowledgment: within 1 business day of receipt.
Initial adjudication: within 5 business days.
Customer callbacks: within 4 business hours of a missed call.
Refund disbursement: funds released within 2 business days of approval.
Dispute resolution target: 10 business days from dispute filing.
Escalated cases: supervisor first-touch within 1 business day.
